## Section 2: Candidate Issues Cleared as False Positives / Sound Design Choices

- **Literature Formulas (L-01 to L-04):**
  - **L-01 (Corwin & Schultz 2012):** Formulas for $\beta = \sum_{j=0}^1 [\ln(H_{t+j}/L_{t+j})]^2$, $\gamma = [\ln(\max(H_t, H_{t+1})/\min(L_t, L_{t+1}))]^2$, $\alpha = (\sqrt{2\beta} - \sqrt{\beta})/(3 - 2\sqrt{2}) - \sqrt{\gamma/(3 - 2\sqrt{2})}$, and spread $S = 2(e^\alpha - 1)/(1 + e^\alpha)$ implemented in `scripts/p2_01_noise.py` match the published paper identically.
  - **L-02 (Abdi & Ranaldo 2017):** Formula using close, high, low with $\eta_t = (h_t + l_t)/2$ and two-period product expectation matches the published paper identically.
  - **L-03 (Bai 1997):** 95% confidence interval critical value of $11.03 \cdot \omega^2 / \delta^2$ corresponds exactly to Bai (1997) Theorem 1 for argmax of two-sided Brownian motion with drift.
  - **L-04 (Liu, Wu & Zidek 1997 / Bai & Perron 2003):** Penalty formulations and criterion choices in `scripts/p2_02_breaks.py` are mathematically sound and consistent with published literature.

- **Continuous 24/7 Crypto Windowing (`scripts/p2_01_noise.py`):**
  - The use of `H.shift(-1)` across UTC midnight is methodologically sound because cryptocurrency markets trade continuously 24/7 without overnight exchange closures, unlike traditional equity markets where overnight gaps require special treatment.

- **Aggregation Consistency Across Scripts:**
  - The ratio of daily 4-hour means in `scripts/p1_02_premium.py` versus the mean of hourly ratios in `scripts/p1_05_donors.py` and `scripts/p2_05_checks.py` differs by less than $0.01$ bps across the sample.
  - Log returns versus linear percentage changes in `scripts/p2_05_checks.py` both round identically to $+0.68\%$ for the 24 Jun 2026 PTAX change.

- **Donor Fixing Windows and Polish Zloty Delisting (`scripts/p1_05_donors.py`):**
  - Donor aggregation windows (ECB snapshot around 14:10 CET vs session averages for COP, UAH, ARS) match each respective central bank's published methodology and trading conventions.
  - Binance `USDTPLN` discontinuation in July 2026 was correctly identified and handled by donor Exclusion Rule 3 in P1-05.
