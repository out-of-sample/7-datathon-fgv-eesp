## 1. Strategy

### 1.1 The thesis (one sentence the jury should repeat)

> **No Brasil, o custo do dólar digital se decide na porta de entrada, não na rede.** Transferir US$ 100 mil em USDT custa
> cerca de 1 USDT; comprá-los no mês seguinte ao ofício de 23/jun custou R$ 4.842 a mais.

"A rede é barata; a porta é cara" is the tagline. It answers the mentor ("how big, who pays, where do the dollars come
from"), speaks to the sponsors' world (Ripple and cross-border payments; stablecoin rails sold on network cost), and every
word is supported: the premium jump is measured (F-016), the fee is a listed 1 USDT (A-02), and the on-chain route paid the
premium too (F-037, exploratory).

### 1.2 What wins here, and our answer

| What the jury scores or asks | Our answer in the deck |
|---|---|
| Relevance (Art. 8) | A Brazilian rule, R$ 106 mi moved in four weeks, US$ 2.4 bi/month market; three more rules arriving now (574, 589, 584) |
| Creativity (Art. 8) | Three markets (exchanges, an on-chain pool, BCB flows), a forensic blockchain ledger, AI agents evaluated as instruments, predictions registered in Git before the data. Team name "Out of Sample", literally |

### 1.4 Design devices (apply on every content slide)

Keep the v3 visual language: navy cover, orange accent, warm off-white pages, Source Serif 4 titles over IBM Plex Sans body,
**blue = ordinary data, orange = the event**. No task or finding IDs on slides (team rule). Then add four devices:

1. **Assertion titles.** The title is the finding in one sentence of at most two lines. A juror who reads only titles gets
   the whole argument (test in §8).
2. **Evidence badge** in the top-right corner, from the four levels agreed on 2 Nov: `Medido` (navy, filled) ·
   `Compatível com` (navy, outline) · `Ilustrativo` (grey, outline) · `Exploratório` (orange, outline). Add "retrospectivo"
   under the badge where it applies. This is what lets us show post-freeze work without overclaiming.
3. **"Como medimos" chip** above the source footer: technique → baseline, in about 12 words. Methods stay visible on every
   results slide, not only on slide 3.
4. **"Limite" line** in grey italic at the bottom: one sentence saying what this slide does not show.

At most three text blocks per slide, one chart that carries the finding, and a source footer. Speaker notes are for the oral
(about 60 s per slide); the PDF must read without them.
