## Research priorities

1. **BRLA:** A single Polygon pool shows a larger dollar-token premium during the same episode using the main study's PTAX window. Treat it as exploratory until swap quality, USDC/USD, a second pool and comparison windows are checked.
2. **Macro spillovers:** The BCB flows and equity price series are available for testing. Investor positions and domestic futures data remain open. The EWZ perpetual comparison is inconclusive due to stale trading.
3. **Market structure:** Use Coinext as attributed licensing background. Confirm the Ludolf referral with David before any follow-up.
4. **Live predictions:** Apply the dated rules in [P3-20](../tasks/P3-20-live-predictions.md), including their windows and placebo references. Distinguish the historical rule comparison from the prospective readout.

## Candidate conclusions

- **Buyer cost:** F-015 translates the acute premium into the cost per US$100k. F-025 scales the extra amount paid against the benchmark using observed turnover. The calculation does not identify recipients, profits, welfare loss or tax revenue. A tariff-equivalent wedge can illustrate the size of the cost.
- **Limits to arbitrage:** F-031 and F-032 document seller-initiated trading on Foxbit during the episode, consistent with price-responsive arbitrage. The traders, funding routes and cause of the fade remain unresolved. F-030's public-source search found no reopening that could be tested.
- **Parallel FX market:** F-024 documents the decline in recorded purchases abroad alongside the premium spike. The market size and the price wedge motivate studying spillovers. Trader motives, substitution and effects on traditional FX or equities require additional evidence.
