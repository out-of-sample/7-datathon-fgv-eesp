## Bottom line

The main fact is now well supported. The premium of USDT over the official dollar jumped on 23–24 Jun 2026. It did so
on three Brazilian exchanges, it is not explained by the real or by USDT's own dollar price, and a break test finds
the date without being told it. Three things are still open:
1. **The cause is unconfirmed.** The 23 Jun BCB notice has a single source, an opinion column.
2. **The jump was mostly temporary.** It was a spike of about one month, and only about +17 bps lasted.
3. **The synthetic control, as planned, cannot pass its own test.** Five comparison currencies are too few for
   the planned p-value.

dollar price, in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%). **Noise band** = how big a premium ordinary trading costs alone would
produce.

## Weak points in the plan (most serious first)

1. **The causal anchor is unverified.** Event E-05 (the 23 Jun BCB notice to banks and funds) rests on one opinion
   column ([`sources.md`](sources.md)). The gate rule says: if it is not confirmed, the jump is presented as "an
   unexplained break". That is still a finding, but a much weaker headline.
2. **The headline number must separate the spike from what lasted.** "Quanto custa fechar a porta?" now has two
   answers: about 1% for one month, then about 0.17%. The planned designs mix them:
   - P2-03's [+1, +20] window covers exactly the spike regime, which lasted 20 trading days;
   - P2-04's post-period, 24 Jun – 30 Sep, averages the spike and what came after;
   - the money result (P3-01) needs both numbers.

   Notes are in the P2-03 and P2-04 Logs. The windows should be fixed **before** running.
3. **The synthetic control cannot pass its own test.** With J comparison currencies, the placebo p-value cannot go
   below 1/(J+1). The current pool (EUR, TRY, ZAR, MXN, COP) gives 0.17. The plan says p > 0.1 falsifies H1, so H1
   could never pass; that needs at least 9 currencies. Binance has no other fiat USDT pair covering Nov 2025 – Sep 2026
   (11 candidates checked). Options are in the [P2-04](../tasks/P2-04-synthetic-control.md) Log:
   - Brazil's own placebo dates (P2-03) as the main inference, with the synthetic control as support;
   - more currencies from other venues (untested);
   - both.
4. **Much of the work is still unreviewed.** P1-05 was checked by an agent working for Renzo, not by a teammate.
   F-007, F-009 and F-012 are "confirmed" but nobody else has re-run them. P2-01 and P2-05 wait for a reviewer. P4-02 needs a
   person who did not produce the numbers.
5. **`[verify]` items still open:**
   - every Brazilian event date (E-01 – E-07);
   - four papers cited from memory (L-01 – L-04);
   - Binance's tick-cut announcement (~27 Jun 2025);

   The 30 Oct briefing notes in `README.md` are still empty.
6. **Many tasks run in parallel.** The full plan stays (see Scope). To protect it, work on the critical path comes
   first whenever two tasks compete for the same person.
7. **Minor:** [P1-09](../tasks/P1-09-donor-history-boundary.md) fixes a 2-bps difference on one date in 2020, outside every
   analysis window. Low priority, P4.
