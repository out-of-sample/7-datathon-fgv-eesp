## Claim
**Feasibility, not a result about an agent.** For one development case (D-01), a script froze 14 public sources with
HTTP 200 and SHA-256. The case: a Brazilian non-financial company buys 100,000 USDT on Foxbit with reais by Pix and
withdraws it to its own wallet on Tron, as of 7 Nov 2026 04:36 UTC. One reader (Renzo's agent) coded 23 conditions;
all 23 quoted passages were found verbatim in the snapshots. Leg and route status were generated by fixed rules. The
**route is not established.** Res. BCB 589 bans banks from operating with unauthorised virtual-asset providers from
6 Nov 2026 and drops the exception for applicants (E-14). Foxbit says it has applied (A-01). No source in the corpus
says whether the company's bank may still send Pix to it (E-20: "não está expressa"). The other three legs are documented,
conditional on the company's own approvals (L2, L3) or supported (L4). Since 1 Nov an external withdrawal also needs a
destination declaration, **even to the client's own wallet** (A-09, A-10).

## Evidence
- 23 condition rows: 13 `availability` (6 supported, 6 conditional, 1 unknown) and 10 `term` (8 supported, 2 unknown,
  kept null: the USDT fee on TRC-20, and execution cost or depth for 100,000 USDT). One term (Res. BCB 584's 24-hour
  hold) is not in force until 1 Jan 2027 and does not count.
- Source access, tested 7 Nov: the Foxbit site, its help-centre API (article created/updated timestamps), the BCB
  normativo API and Tether returned 200. Binance returned 202 (bot challenge). Mercado Bitcoin returned 403. Avenia
  returned 200. A June 2026 copy exists only for some documents (Foxbit terms of use 10 Jun; Binance fee page 15 Jun;
  Avenia terms 16 Feb and 7 Sep). Details in `results/P3-28_feasibility.md` §3.
- Reproduce:
  ```bash
  .venv/bin/python scripts/p3_28_access_map.py selftest   # aggregation rules
  .venv/bin/python scripts/p3_28_access_map.py card       # offline: passage check + results/tables/P3-28_access_cards.md
  .venv/bin/python scripts/p3_28_access_map.py snapshot   # network: only sources without a successful snapshot
  ```
  The raw snapshots are in `data/raw/p3_28/` (out of git; checksums in `data/raw/SHA256SUMS`, manifest
  `data/raw/p3_28/manifest.csv`). A teammate without them runs `snapshot` first. Pages may have changed since, so the
  passage check can fail. That failure is the expected signal, not a bug.

## Caveats
- **One coder, one case, development only.** These are not reference labels and measure no method. Nothing here
  supports H1, that an agent beats a simpler workflow.
- "Not established" describes the frozen documents. It is not a finding that Pix to Foxbit fails, and not a legal
  opinion on Res. 589. A BCB applicant list, a bank's or Foxbit's statement, or an observed deposit could resolve it;
  none is in the corpus.
- The coding choices are open to a second reader: Foxbit's stablecoin review (coded as conditional, could be
  conflicting); "BitGo/Fireblocks" custody (which custodian is not stated).
- Provider pages carry no version date. Foxbit's fee API timestamp is a cache time. Retrospective mode only: nothing
  here describes what a company could do in June.
- The case is a company, not an investment fund. It says nothing about the funds' FX channel the 23 Jun notice stopped
  (F-030).
