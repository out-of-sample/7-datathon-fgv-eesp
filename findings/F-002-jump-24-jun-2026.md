## Claim
The day after a reported BCB notice to banks and funds (23 Jun 2026), the premium rose from 16 bps to 144 bps
and stayed near 100 bps for weeks. Three other rule dates (Res. 519-521 published, Res. 521 in force,
reporting obligation) show no comparable move.

## Evidence
Mean premium (bps), 30 days before vs after:

| Date | Event | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-05-22 | IOF decree (E-01) | 30.9 | 44.3 |
| 2025-11-10 | BCB Res. 519-521 published | 15.2 | 7.8 |
| 2026-02-02 | BCB Res. 521 in force | 1.5 | 0.3 |
| 2026-05-04 | Reporting obligation (E-04) | 7.1 | 6.6 |
| 2026-06-23 | BCB notice to banks/funds (E-05) | 7.5 | 95.2 |

Daily (unadjusted probe values; see F-009 for USDT/USD-adjusted v2 values): 16 (23 Jun), 144 (24 Jun), 172 (25 Jun), 64 (26 Jun), 127 (29 Jun), 113 (1 Jul), 177 (3 Jul).
A Conjur column (1 Jul 2026) reports the stablecoin spread rising from ~0.1% to ~0.7% after the notice.

Reproduce: `python scripts/00_probe_premium.py ...` (event table and the 15 Jun - 3 Jul daily listing).

## Caveats
The event date comes from one opinion column (P1-04 must confirm it). PTAX rose ~0.7% on 24 Jun
(5.1743 -> 5.2098) before reverting; the premium did not revert (P2-05 checks this). The hypotheses were
written after seeing these numbers: this is motivation, not confirmation (P2-02 and P2-04 are confirmatory).
